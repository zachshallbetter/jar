Set up your box

Name yourself, add the household, pair your phones. Everything stays on the box.

Open this page from the link on the box's sticker; it carries the box's setup key.

1 You

You'll be the box's owner: the one who can add people and devices later.

2 The household

Adults see the house; kids see their own things; a household device is a shared screen like a TV.

    3 Pair phones

    On the phone, open Jellybean: it finds the box on your Wi-Fi. Enter the code, or open this page on that phone and tap the link. Codes last 10 minutes.

    4 Whose calendar is whose

    After pairing, each phone shares its calendars with the box. Name the calendars that belong to each person (comma separated).

      5 Recovery phrase

      Backups of the box are locked with a phrase only you keep. It's shown once: write it down and keep it somewhere safe, away from the box. Without it, a backup can't be opened.

      6 Connect the house

      The box runs Home Assistant for your lights, thermostat and the rest. This sets it up for you. Locks, alarms, garage doors and cameras are listed but never controlled from here.

      7 Done

      Setup closes by itself once your own phone is paired. Finishing now also deletes the setup key.

      Your box is set up

      Open Jellybean on your phone: adding people and devices lives in Settings › Household.
